Method

  • Data source: NASA POWER (Prediction Of Worldwide Energy Resources), monthly & annual point data, parameter PRECTOTCORR_SUM — MERRA-2 bias-corrected total precipitation, 1 Jan 2000 – 31 Dec 2025.
  • Series: the annual total (mm) for each of 26 calendar years in each of Türkiye's 81 provinces. No values were missing.
  • Points: one NASA POWER point query per province. İstanbul, Ankara, İzmir, Konya, Trabzon and Antalya use the city-centre coordinates; the other 75 use the geometric centre of the province polygon. The coordinates are listed in the table.
  • Trend size: Theil–Sen slope (median of all pairwise year-to-year slopes), robust to single extreme years. "25-year trend" = slope × 25, the change along the trend line from 2000 to 2025.
  • Percent change: the 25-year trend divided by the trend-line value in 2000.
  • Significance: two-sided Mann–Kendall test (normal approximation with continuity and tie correction). A trend is labelled significant when p < 0.05.
  • All statistics are computed in your browser from the data embedded in this page.

Limitations

  • Not station data. POWER precipitation comes from the MERRA-2 reanalysis on a ~0.5° × 0.625° grid (roughly 50 km). Each "site" is a grid-cell average and can differ from local rain gauges, especially near coasts and mountains.
  • Short record. 26 years is short for precipitation, which varies strongly from year to year; a real trend of this size can easily fail to reach significance, and a few wet or dry years at either end can shift the slope.
  • One point per province. A single grid cell stands in for a whole province; large or mountainous provinces contain very different rainfall regimes that this misses.
  • Many tests at once. With 81 provinces tested at p < 0.05, about four would look "significant" by chance alone even with no real trend, and no multiple-testing correction was applied. Neighbouring provinces also share weather systems, so their results are not independent pieces of evidence.
  • Annual totals only. Seasonal shifts, rainfall intensity and drought duration are not analysed; neither is evaporation, so this is not a full water-balance picture.
  • Test assumptions. Mann–Kendall assumes independent years; serial correlation was not corrected for.
  • Trends describe 2000–2025 only and are not forecasts or attribution to any cause.
